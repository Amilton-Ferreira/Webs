About us

Africell is a fast gorwing pan-African mobile network operator

We provide fast and realible mobile network coverage and related technology services to more than 16 milion subscribers in Sub-Sahara Africa.

Trough a mix voice and SMS products, data services, And Afrimoney(our mobile money plataform), we help customers across the African continent share in the extraordinary benefits of digital connectivity.

What we do

We offer customers realiable, afordable and inovative telcommunications products and services

Voice & SMS

Africell's voice and services SMS are amoung the best value in the market and are relied on by millions of people our handsets suits every customers type and can be used for voice, data and more

Data

Data is the heartbeat of Africell's network. We offer a range of internet services including ultrafast 4G and 5G connectivity defined by speed, realiable and value-for-money

Mobile money

Trough Afrimoney, mobile users can send money to others, pay bills, and transact with a range of other products and services-both at home and overseas
